# Photon on b1.7.3 (Phase 7)

The Phase 7 target (development.md, owner requirement 7): **Photon v1.4, High, ≥ 60 FPS average and ≥ 45 FPS 1% low**
on the owner's machine, GTX 1650 Ti via PRIME, 1920×1080, render distance 16 chunks, the day scene's orbit.

**Setup:** scene `bench_target` (`tools/testscene/scenes/`: `bench_orbit` at 1920×1080 with Altrender's render
distance at 16 chunks, the game's step at Far): an orbit 16 blocks around the overworld_day camera, a warm-up lap,
then 10 s measured; Fancy, smooth lighting, `ar_debug` textures. Photon's default options are its High profile.
`-Daltrender.debug.gpuTimers` for the per-pass GPU times (F3's GPU breakdown: `terrain0` = opaque and cut-out terrain
with the pack's programs, `terrain1` = translucent terrain, `shadow` = the shadow pass, `pack` = the pack's
deferred, composite and final passes; entities, sky, weather, clouds and hand are their own rows and add up to
under 0.1 ms here). The laptop idle, on power.

## Baseline (2026-11-14, before Phase 7's changes)

Three Photon runs interleaved with two without a pack, then one on the Intel GPU.

| Run | Avg FPS | 1% low FPS | p50 frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| Photon, NVIDIA (3 runs) | 49.6 (49.1–50.2) | 40.4 (39.0–41.1) | 21.5 ms |
| No pack, NVIDIA (2 runs) | 670 | 231 | 1.45 ms |
| Photon, Intel UHD | 5.3 | 4.4 | 203 ms |

GPU time per frame, Photon on NVIDIA (average of the three runs' last timer windows):

| Pass | ms |
|---|---|
| terrain0 (opaque and cut-out terrain) | 1.42 |
| terrain1 (translucent terrain: water drawn twice, depth then colour) | 5.18 (4.4–5.6) |
| shadow | 2.24 |
| pack (deferred, composite, final) | 9.36 (9.0–9.5) |
| everything else | < 0.1 |
| **GPU frame** | **18.6** |

The frame's 21.5 ms (p50) is above the GPU's 18.6 ms: the PRIME copy to the display GPU and the CPU's share come on
top. The render thread waits for the GPU outside the buffer swap on NVIDIA (the swap takes 0.015 ms), so the CPU
frame time is measured as the render thread's own CPU time (`bench_render_thread_cpu_ms`, from the next runs on).
On Intel the wait is in the swap (178 ms of 203) and the render thread's work is 10.2 ms a frame.

**Photon on modern Iris** (development.md 0.8; the owner, 2026-11-14): about **50 FPS** on High in Minecraft 26.3
(Iris 1.11.7, Sodium 0.9.2) on the same laptop, read in game (not the bench harness: settings as the owner plays).
Getting 26.3's OpenGL backend to start needed `-Dorg.lwjgl.opengl.contextAPI=native` on this Wayland session (LWJGL
picked EGL while SDL fell back to XWayland/GLX).

## After the water depth pass, shadow culling and the GPU-driven shadow pass (2026-11-14)

Development.md 7.2/7.3: the translucent terrain's depth-only draw (fancy water) takes a depth-only twin of the pack's
water program (its vertex shader, an alpha-test-only fragment shader) instead of the full one; the shadow pass culls
as Iris does (`shadow.culling`; Photon's default: only sections whose shadow can reach the view) and draws its opaque
terrain through the GPU-driven path; the light handoff per entity no longer resets the GL state cache. Each of the
first two switched off once (`-Daltshaders.debug.noWaterDepth`, `-Daltrender.debug.shadowCulling.disable`) to show
its share. Same setup and pack as the baseline.

| Run | Avg FPS | 1% low FPS | p50 frame | GPU frame | terrain1 | shadow | Shadow sections (opaque / translucent) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All on (3 runs) | **61.5** (61.0–62.1) | **49.5** (48.1–50.5) | 16.9 ms | 15.4 ms | 2.85 ms | 1.28 ms | 534 / 173 |
| Without the water depth twin | 52.7 | 43.0 | 20.3 ms | 18.4 ms | 5.71 ms | 1.28 ms | 533 / 172 |
| Without shadow culling | 56.8 | 47.7 | 18.2 ms | 15.6 ms | 2.27 ms | 2.14 ms | 1213 / 425 |

terrain0 (1.4 ms) and the pack's passes (9.2–9.6 ms) are unchanged. **The target holds in these three runs** (60 / 45),
with a thin margin. The render thread's CPU time is 15.7–16.1 ms a frame, close to the frame: NVIDIA's driver
spin-waits for the GPU by default, so this doesn't tell work from waiting yet.

## The solid/cut-out split (2026-11-14)

The opaque layer's quads whose sprite has no see-through texel drawn with Photon's `gbuffers_terrain_solid` (no
alpha test), the rest with `gbuffers_terrain`; the shadow pass likewise (development.md 7.3). Switched off once with
`-Daltrender.debug.cutoutSplit.disable`.

| Run | Avg FPS | 1% low FPS | p50 frame | GPU frame | terrain0 | terrain1 | shadow |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Split on (3 runs) | 61.2 (60.9–61.6) | 50.8 (49.9–51.4) | 16.9 ms | 15.4 ms | 1.42 ms | 2.82 ms | 1.31 ms |
| Split off | 61.2 | 48.4 | 16.9 ms | 15.6 ms | 1.39 ms | 2.94 ms | 1.31 ms |

**No measurable gain here:** Photon's terrain shading is cheap at this view (1.4 ms for the opaque layer either way)
and its shadow programs discard on their own. Kept: it is Iris's split (solid blocks through `terrain_solid`), costs
nothing, and may matter with heavier opaque overdraw or packs; a smaller pack vertex (the plan's last Altrender item)
isn't done, as nothing here shows the terrain passes are vertex-bound.

**Where the frame goes now** (GPU 15.4 ms): the pack's deferred/composite/final passes 9.4 ms (61 %), translucent
terrain 2.8, opaque terrain 1.4, shadow 1.3; the frame's p50 is 16.9 ms (PRIME copy and CPU on top). The rest of
Phase 7's headroom is AltShaders' (7.4).

## After the Iris comparison fixes and 7.4 (2026-11-14, target check)

Same setup. Four Photon runs interleaved with two without a pack, one with 7.4's savings off
(`-Daltshaders.debug.no74`: every uniform sent, mipmaps made at every use), then Intel with and without them.
Photon now gets its sky light (the `colorimg4` binding; its shade was near black before), which costs its passes
about 0.3 ms more.

## Water, shadow back faces, copies (2026-11-14)

Same setup, Photon High on NVIDIA, the runs interleaved: four with fancy water drawn once (its depth draw skipped,
now `-Daltshaders.debug.skipWaterDepthDraw`), two with the depth draw first (the default), and with the water drawn
once: one with every shadow face's back culled, one without AltShaders' copies after `final` (timing only: wrong
history), one with the driver asked to yield (`__GL_YIELD=USLEEP`).

| Run | Avg FPS | 1% low FPS | GPU frame | terrain1 | shadow | pack | Render thread CPU |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Water drawn once (4 runs) | 60.7 (60.2–61.5) | 49.7 | 15.78 ms | 2.91 ms | 1.28 ms | 9.72 ms | 16.1 ms |
| Water's depth drawn first (2 runs) | 60.2 (59.8–60.5) | 48.6 | 15.78 ms | 2.91 ms | 1.28 ms | 9.68 ms | 16.2 ms |
| Shadow back faces culled (every face) | 60.9 | 50.5 | 15.65 ms | 2.91 ms | 1.12 ms | 9.75 ms | 16.0 ms |
| No copies after `final` | 61.4 | 49.9 | 15.46 ms | 2.75 ms | 1.30 ms | 9.47 ms | 15.9 ms |
| Driver yielding | 60.2 | 49.0 | 15.98 ms | 2.97 ms | 1.30 ms | 9.82 ms | 15.4 ms |

The water's depth draw costs nothing measurable (the translucent terrain takes 2.91 ms either way: what the draw
saves comes back as more water shading behind the nearest surface), so it stays. Culling every shadow face's back
saves 0.16 ms of the shadow pass but speckles leaves' shadows; the option culls the solid quads' backs only (not
timed; less than that). The copies after `final` (Iris's swap passes) are about 0.25 ms of the pack's 9.7. The
render thread's CPU time stays near the frame time on NVIDIA even with the driver yielding: its waits aren't
separable there (7.2 uses Intel's figure). The runs drift slower over the batch (61.5 → 60.2 for the same build).
